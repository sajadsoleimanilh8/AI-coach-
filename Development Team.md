## Collaboration

SportsStrategyCoachAI is developed through a collaborative engineering
workflow covering research, implementation, testing, integration, and
validation.

responsible for the architecture, AI/ML development, computer-vision pipeline,

All team members contribute to the development and refinement of the platform
across their respective areas.
